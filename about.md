Cardiometabolic diseases, such as type 2 diabetes (T2D) and cardiovascular disease (CVD), are a major burden on public health systems. Efficient risk prediction and prevention can improve population health.

## Why glycans?

The glycome, the set of carbohydrate chains attached to proteins, modulates chronic inflammation. It is dynamic and changes in response to lifestyle and pharmacological interventions, which makes it a promising biomarker for prevention and personalised intervention.

## Aim

GlyXpred aims to improve the prediction of cardiometabolic disease risk with machine learning models that use glycome data as input, and to identify lifestyle patterns that reduce risk and biological age. A key aim is interpretability, so that healthcare professionals and patients can make informed, data-driven decisions.

## Background

GlyXpred continues work started in the Horizon Europe project [SynHealth](https://synhealth-project.eu/), from which the intervention study cohort originates.
