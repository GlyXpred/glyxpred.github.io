## Data and open science

The project follows the principle "as open as possible, as closed as necessary". Cohort and intervention study data are personal health data protected under GDPR, so they are not redistributed by the project. Where consent and ethics approvals allow, non-identifiable outputs (models, code, documentation) are shared with persistent identifiers (DOI).
